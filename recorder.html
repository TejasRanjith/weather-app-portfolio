Recording Instructions
1
Click Start Capture Session. Your browser will ask you to select a screen, window, or tab. Select the tab or window displaying your running Weather App. Check the "Share system audio" option if you'd like to capture audio outputs.
2
Click Record Demo to start recording. You will see a live counter timer in the corner showing that recording is active. Speak into your microphone to explain both your code and the output results.
3
Click Stop & Save Video when done. The recorded video file will compile instantly and download to your machine as `weather_app_demo.webm`.
4
Upload the resulting video to your Google Drive, YouTube, or Vimeo account, and paste the viewable URL in your final submission portal.
